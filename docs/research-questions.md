# Open research questions

The Commons should remain open to learning rather than defending a fixed security thesis.

- Which containment boundaries most often change outcomes across organization types?
- Where do recovery plans fail even when preventive controls look strong?
- Which controls are easiest for small organizations to verify rather than merely claim?
- Which incident scenarios are most useful for non-specialist tabletop exercises?
- How should we represent uncertainty without making the tool unusable?
- Can a simple dependency graph expose high-leverage single points of failure without pretending to be a full asset inventory?
- What defensive patterns can cities, schools, hospitals, NGOs and small businesses safely share with each other?

Future evidence should be allowed to change the simulator, taxonomy, priorities, and even the product direction.
